Room XI Connect

Organization & City Partner Experience

For Youth-Serving Organizations

Room XI Connect provides privacy-safe analytics, QR attendance tracking, and real-time insights for youth-serving organizations and city partners. All data is aggregated and anonymized - you never see individual youth data.

🔒 Privacy-First Data

All organization-facing data uses k-anonymity (minimum 7 users per bucket) and differential privacy (mathematical noise). You get actionable insights without compromising individual privacy.

What Organizations Get

📊

Real-Time Analytics

Live dashboards showing user engagement, check-in trends, and program popularity.

📱

QR Attendance

Replace paper sign-in sheets with instant QR scanning. Export reports for funders.

⚠️

Crisis Monitoring

Aggregate crisis pattern data (not individual conversations) for community needs assessment.

👥

Demographics Insights

Understand who you're serving with aggregated, equity-focused demographic data.

👥 Demographics & Equity

Youth self-report their identity privately. You see aggregate distributions to identify underserved populations and equity gaps.

Self-Reported Data

  • Age distribution
  • Gender identity
  • Sexual orientation
  • Racial/ethnic identity
  • Indigenous self-identification
  • Disability status

Equity Analysis

  • Compare to census demographics
  • Identify underserved groups
  • Track representation over time
  • Guardian perception comparison

⚠️ Crisis Monitoring

Privacy Note

Organizations never see individual conversations or crisis details. You only see aggregate patterns (counts, timing, resolution rates) to understand community needs.

  • Total crisis flags detected (count only)
  • Crisis resolution rate
  • Peak crisis times (for staffing decisions)
  • Trend data (increasing/decreasing)

🛡️ Privacy & Compliance

Alberta Law Compliance

  • PIPA: Dual-layer consent, audit trails
  • HIA: Health data handled carefully
  • Under-16: Guardian verification system
  • OIPC: 72-hour breach notification

Privacy Protections

  • K-anonymity (min 7 users per bucket)
  • Differential privacy (Laplace noise)
  • H3 geo-bucketing for location
  • End-to-end encryption
