Feature Details

Youth Experience

🌈 Daily Mood Check-ins

How It Works

  • Tap the Mood Orb on your home screen
  • Select how you're feeling (1-6 scale)
  • Choose emotion tags that resonate
  • Add an optional note about your day
  • Watch your Mood Orb evolve over time

Features

  • 6-level mood scale (struggling → thriving)
  • Affect tags to describe emotions
  • 7-day Mood Orb visualization
  • Streak tracking for habit building
  • Export your Mood Orb as an image

📍 Program Discovery

Program Categories

  • Mental Health & Counseling
  • LGBTQ+ & 2Spirit Support
  • Indigenous Programs
  • Arts & Creative
  • Sports & Recreation
  • Employment & Career
  • Crisis Support

Features

  • "Today" tab shows current programs
  • Filter by age, cost, drop-in
  • Map view for nearby programs
  • Save programs for later
  • Direct contact info for each

💬 Ximi AI Companion

Important Note

Ximi is not a therapist or crisis counselor. It's a supportive AI companion that can offer coping strategies and connect you to real resources. If you're in crisis, Ximi will immediately connect you to Kids Help Phone and other emergency services.

Ximi Can Help With

  • Talking through tough emotions
  • Suggesting coping strategies
  • Finding local programs
  • Connecting to crisis support

Safety Features

  • Crisis keyword detection
  • Immediate escalation when needed
  • "Not a clinician" disclaimer
  • Available 24/7

Privacy & Getting Started

Youth Experience

🛡️ Your Privacy, Your Control

Room XI Connect is built for Alberta privacy law (PIPA). You decide what to share, and you can change your mind anytime.

Consent Toggles

All sharing is OFF by default. You choose what to enable.

Data Export

Download all your data as JSON anytime.

Account Deletion

Delete your account and all data with one tap.

Youth Autonomy

Your demographics are never shared with parents.

No Advertisers. No Data Selling. Ever.

We don't sell your data to advertisers or share it with data brokers. Your information is used only to provide you with support and connect you to programs.

📱 QR Attendance

Sign up once, attend anywhere. When you visit a program, just scan their QR code - no paper forms needed.

  • Universal intake: one signup works across all programs
  • Privacy-preserving: uses anonymous ID for attendance
  • No paperwork at each program visit

🆘 Crisis Support

Available 24/7, even without logging in.

Emergency Resources

  • Kids Help Phone: 1-800-668-6868
  • CMHA Distress Line: 780-482-4357
  • 211 Alberta: Dial 211 for service navigation
  • Emergency: 911
