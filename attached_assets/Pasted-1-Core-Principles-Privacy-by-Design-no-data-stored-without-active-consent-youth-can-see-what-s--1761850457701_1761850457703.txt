1. Core Principles

Privacy by Design — no data stored without active consent; youth can see what’s collected and revoke any time.

Age Gating — auto-triggers guardian consent under 16 (Alberta FOIP/PIPA alignment).

Plain-Language Consent Cards — each screen summarized in youth-friendly text with “Read More” for legal language.

Session-Scoped Data — non-essential emotional data (mood check-ins, journals) stored locally and deleted after 30 days unless the youth opts in to sync.

2. Multi-Step Flow

Step 1: Youth Acknowledgement
Youth reads short statements about privacy, safety, and data sharing.

Step 2: Guardian Consent (if required)

Generates secure guardian token link (via email or SMS).

Guardian reviews data-use summary and approves with PIN.

Step 3: Program Partner Consent (optional)
If a youth connects to a partner organization (e.g. YMCA site), that partner gets scoped access only to program attendance and activity data.

Step 4: Withdrawal and Review Rights
From the Me tab → Privacy section, youth and guardians can view, export, or delete data. This triggers an automated erasure job via Supabase Edge Function.

3. Technical Safeguards

XID (Hash Identifier) per user to unlink personal data from program records.

Canadian Data Residency — stored only on Supabase (CA region).

Guardian Audit Trail — timestamped approvals stored in consents table with JWT-based verification.

No Cross-Tenant Access — organizations can view only their own youth through Row Level Security (RLS).

4. Legal Compliance

Aligned with PIPA (Alberta), FOIP, and PHIPA (Ontario) standards.

Mirrored UNICEF AI for Children guidelines and Canadian Council on Privacy and Access to Information’s Youth Data Use Framework.

5. UX Presentation

Consent prompts appear in a calm modal with Room XI’s sage and gold palette.

Clear buttons: “Agree & Continue”, “Ask a Question”, “See What’s Shared”.

For low-literacy users, text-to-speech is available.

6. Administrative Controls

Staff Dashboard shows consent status per youth (ID verified ✅ / Guardian Pending ⚠️).

Exportable audit reports for funders or privacy audits without revealing personal data.
