🌈 ROOM XI CONNECT — MOOD ORB SYSTEM

⸻

🧭 PURPOSE

The Mood Orb is a slow-changing, ambient visual that reflects a youth’s emotional tone across the past 7 days — without labels, numbers, or judgment.
It gives back a visual sense of balance, not a diagnosis.

“A mirror, not a measurement.”

⸻

🎯 CORE PRINCIPLES

Principle	What it Means
All moods visible	The Orb always blends all 6 tones — never just shows “one mood”
No spikes or slices	It’s not a pie chart. It blends gently over time.
Slow settle	Changes fade in over 10–12 minutes, not instantly
Ambient and private	No text labels, no sharing unless opted in
Calm design	Gentle visuals that work on low-end devices with accessibility support


⸻

🌈 THE 6 MOOD TONES

Mood	Color	Meaning
clear	Gold	Calm, centered
breezy	Coral	Expressive, lighthearted
aurora	Teal	Focused, energized
foggy	Sage	Reflective, inward
stormy	Indigo-grey	Heavy, stuck
cold	Blue-violet	Low energy, withdrawn


⸻

🧮 DATA INPUT

Orb visuals are driven by the check-in summary API:

API: GET /api/checkins/summary?window=7

Example response:

{
  "ratios": {
    "clear": 0.42,
    "foggy": 0.15,
    "breezy": 0.13,
    "aurora": 0.10,
    "stormy": 0.10,
    "cold": 0.10
  },
  "variance": 0.38,
  "streak7": 5,
  "dominant": "clear"
}


⸻

🧱 VISUAL LAYERS

Layer	Function
Base gradients	Radial gradients for each color tone, opacity mapped to ratio
Breathing motion	Subtle 8s animation loop (scale + brightness)
Streak halo	Outer glow intensity increases with streak (0–7)
Ambient background	Page softly tinted toward dominant color
Pattern overlays	Optional: Dots (warm) and lines (cool) for color-blind accessibility

    stormy: 0.10,
    cold: 0.10
  }}
  variance={0.38}
  streak7={5}
  dominant="clear"
  settleMs={720000} // 12 minutes
/>


⸻

🌀 SETTLE ANIMATION LOGIC

Goal	Visual updates interpolate slowly, not snap in


1. When a check-in is completed:
	•	The Orb gets new ratios
	•	It stores:
	•	orbTarget = new ratios
	•	tweenStartAt = timestamp of update
	•	The Orb starts interpolating current → target over settleMs

2. Interpolation uses:
	•	requestAnimationFrame
	•	Easing: easeInOutQuad
	•	Opacity vars update gradually

3. If prefers-reduced-motion is on:
	•	Skip animation or reduce to 2 minutes

⸻

📦 LOCAL PERSISTENCE
	•	Orb tween state (orbTarget, tweenStartAt) is saved to localStorage
	•	On app reopen:
	•	Tween resumes from saved progress
	•	Prevents “jump” in Orb visuals
	•	No sensitive data (e.g. mood check-in timestamps) is stored locally

⸻

♿ ACCESSIBILITY & SETTINGS

Feature	Detail
✅ High-visibility toggle	Adds outline, more defined halo
✅ Pattern overlay	Optional toggle: dots for warm hues, lines for cool
✅ Color key drawer	“What do the colors mean?” — opens a legend panel
✅ Reduced motion	Shortens or disables animations
✅ Keyboard nav	Orb and drawer are keyboard-focusable
✅ Text contrast	Follows WCAG AA over Orb background


⸻

🧘 FALLBACK BEHAVIOR

Case	Behavior
No data from API	Show “Orb is resting — check in to reflect”
No check-ins in 7 days	Show muted orb with low opacity (balanced gray base)
User disables Orb	Hide component; optionally show static message
Consent revoked	Orb disables instantly with message: “Reflection disabled until consent is restored”
Offline	Orb uses last-synced ratios and settles locally


⸻

📊 WEEKLY SNAPSHOT

Feature	Description
✅ This Week vs Last	Shows ratios + streak7 comparison
✅ Avg Mood Marker	Chip with most prominent ratio
✅ Time-lapse	Optional 30-day replay (1 orb per day)
✅ Export	.gif or .mp4, opt-in and disabled by default


⸻

🔐 PRIVACY RULES
	•	No raw moods ever exposed to client — only 7-day summary
	•	No timestamps or locations are passed into Orb
	•	Ambient background tint is a frontend-only effect
	•	Orb never appears on public views unless youth has opted in (e.g. Mood Drop, dual-view with staff)

⸻

✅ ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
	•	Orb reflects all 6 moods at all times
	•	Settles over 10–12 min unless reduced motion is on
	•	localStorage saves orbTarget and tweenStartAt
	•	Accessibility passes: WCAG AA, reduced motion, pattern overlay
	•	No missing data crashes UI
	•	Weekly Snapshot + Time-lapse fully integrated
	•	Consent toggles affect Orb instantly
	•	Mood ratios are rendered using CSS opacity vars and blend modes

⸻

💡 WHY THIS WORKS
	•	✅ Respects emotional nuance: Everyone has mixed feelings — Orb shows the blend, not a label.
	•	✅ Calm interaction: No streak pressure, no charts, just ambient color.
	•	✅ Reflective, not reactive: Slowness is a feature — gives space between input and reflection.
	•	✅ Privacy-first: No individual timestamps, locations, or tracking behavior ever exposed.

⸻

🔚 TL;DR: How the Mood Orb Works

It’s a layered color orb that blends 6 moods based on your last 7 days of check-ins. It breathes gently. It settles slowly when your moods shift. It never labels you. It remembers where it was and picks up gently. It reflects how you’ve been, not what you are.
