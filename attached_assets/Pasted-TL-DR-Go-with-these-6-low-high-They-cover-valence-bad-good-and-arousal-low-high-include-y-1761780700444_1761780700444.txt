TL;DR: Go with these 6 (low → high). They cover valence (bad→good) and arousal (low→high), include your Cold, and avoid overlap:
	1.	Cold ❄️ — Numb, withdrawn, low energy
	2.	Stormy ⛈️ — Overwhelmed, heavy feelings
	3.	Foggy 🌫️ — Unclear, confused, meh
	4.	Clear ☀️ — Calm, stable, doing okay
	5.	Breezy ⚡ — Upbeat, energized, motivated
	6.	Aurora 🌌 — Amazing, glowing, best mood

Why this set: distinct buckets across negative/positive + low/high energy; “Cold” captures low-valence/low-arousal separately from “Stormy” (high-arousal negative). “Foggy” covers confusion without being negative. “Clear” is the neutral-good anchor. “Breezy” is high-energy positive. “Aurora” handles awe/peak days.
