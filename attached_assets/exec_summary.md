# Room XI Connect — Executive Summary

Room XI Connect is a trauma-informed, privacy-first youth reflection ecosystem. It blends a 7-day Mood Orb gradient, opt-in AI reflection (Ximi), Explore program discovery, and anonymized community insights. The platform is non-diagnostic, compliant with Canadian privacy law, and designed for schools and youth organizations to understand group climate without surveillance. Timezone America/Edmonton. Writing follows Room XI rules, no em dashes.
